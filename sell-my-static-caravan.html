Statics without the stress

We deal with the awkward bits

Selling a static is rarely as simple as selling a tourer — there are parks, pitches, site rules and transport to think about. We handle statics regularly, so tell us where it sits and what the situation is, and we will tell you honestly what we can do.

Based in Lancashire and out on the road most days, we buy, sell and trade caravans across the whole of the UK — if the van is right, distance is never a problem.
