Meet the owner

Straight talking, no messing

Flog My Caravan is run by Tom. The man who values your van is the man who turns up and pays you.

Who you are dealing with

One person, start to finish

Flog My Caravan is run by Tom. When you call the number on this page, that is who you speak to — not a call centre, not a middleman. He values the van, he makes the offer, and he brings the money.

Tom has been buying, towing and trading caravans for the best part of fifteen years, a family hobby that turned into a full time job. He started Flog My Caravan after watching too many sellers get messed about online: ghosted buyers, silly offers and no-shows. His way is simpler — one honest figure and a handshake.

Based in Lancashire and out on the road most days, we buy, sell and trade caravans across the whole of the UK — if the van is right, distance is never a problem.
