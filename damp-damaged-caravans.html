No van too tired

Honest offers on honest problems

A damp reading or a smashed panel wipes out most private buyers overnight, and dealers will not touch a problem van at all. We will. Be upfront about what is wrong, send photos of the damage along with the rest, and the offer you get is realistic and reliable — no dramatic chip-down when we arrive.

Based in Lancashire and out on the road most days, we buy, sell and trade caravans across the whole of the UK — if the van is right, distance is never a problem.
