One van or a whole yard — multiple caravans bought and moved in a single visit, anywhere in the UK.

Bulk made simple

One visit, everything gone

Storage yards, farms, dealerships and parks often need several vans gone at once — end of season, change of use, or simply reclaiming the space. We buy in bulk, bring the right transport, and clear the lot in one organised visit rather than a trickle of separate buyers.

Based in Lancashire and out on the road most days, we buy, sell and trade caravans across the whole of the UK — if the van is right, distance is never a problem.
